The Boutique for Your Grace & Glamour

Where every thread tells a story of elegance.

Welcome to SK Glitz & Glam, a premium fashion boutique based in Hyderabad, specializing in signature Maggam work, advanced computer embroidery, and a meticulously curated collection of sarees.

Founded by Ms Kumarapu Sridevi, a passionate Fashion Designer hailing from the prestigious International Institute of Fashion Design & Interior Design (INIFD) Pune, the boutique was born from a desire to blend traditional Indian artisanship with contemporary fashion sensibilities. Our clothes are more than just garments; they are wearable art.

Her journey is backed not only by creativity but also by formal training. As reflected in her INIFD Pune certificate, Sridevi successfully completed her Fashion Design course during the 2016-2017 session, strengthening her foundation in design thinking, garment styling, and boutique craftsmanship. That academic grounding continues to shape the thoughtful detailing and refined finish seen across every SK Glitz & Glam creation.

We pride ourselves on our impeccable attention to detail. Whether it's the intricate zardosi beadwork of our Maggam blouses, the flawless precision of our machine embroidery, or the rich heritage woven into our curated sarees, we ensure that you step out in grace and glamour.

At SK Glitz & Glam, we believe that luxury lies in craftsmanship. Our boutique is designed to be a haven for brides, mothers, and occasion-wear shoppers looking for that perfect, personalized touch.
