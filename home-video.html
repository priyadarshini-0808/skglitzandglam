Welcome to SK Glitz and Glam

Welcome to SK Glitz and Glam, where elegance blends seamlessly with tradition. Founded by Kumarapu Sridevi, whose passion for fashion began with childhood sketches and later evolved through formal training at INIFD Pune, our boutique is known for bespoke designs, detailed handwork, and custom blouses worn even by celebrities. We prioritize understanding each client’s unique style and preferences, offering personalized services to craft outfits that truly resonate. Alongside our custom creations, we present a thoughtfully curated collection of sarees and apparel for the entire family. To make styling effortless for everyone, we also provide ready-to-wear sarees—ensuring a flawless drape with ease and confidence.
